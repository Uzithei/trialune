# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. These rules
apply to project issues, reviews, discussions and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive. Critique code and ideas rather than people.
- Make room for different perspectives and accept corrections with care.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal information, credentials or private conversations.
- Give appropriate credit and contribute only material you have permission to share.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing and
deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Repeatedly ignoring moderation requests is also a
violation.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact a maintainer through an available private project channel. If no private channel
is listed, ask for one publicly without identifying the affected person or describing the
incident. Report security vulnerabilities according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, consider the context and explain their decision
to those involved where safe. They may request corrections, remove content, issue a warning,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation depending on severity and repetition.
Share report details only with people who need them to address the incident.

A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration and provide relevant
context through the same private channel. A maintainer directly involved in an incident
should defer review to an uninvolved maintainer when one is available.
